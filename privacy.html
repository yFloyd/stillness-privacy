Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

MonsTation built the Stillness: Minimalist Launcher app as a Freemium application. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Stillness: Minimalist Launcher is designed with privacy in mind. All launcher preferences, favorite applications, and daily focus tasks are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal usage data on external servers.

2. Installed Applications Permission

To function as an Android home screen launcher, the app queries the list of installed applications on your device solely to display and launch them locally. This list is never transmitted off your device.

3. In-App Purchases

We use Google Play Billing and RevenueCat to process and validate optional Pro upgrades. Payment transactions are handled securely by Google Play, and we do not have access to your credit card or billing details.

4. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact MonsTation via the developer support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
